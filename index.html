Ruize Richard Xu

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Chicago, advised by Zhiyuan Li, with research at TTIC.

I am interested in coding agents, reinforcement learning, formal theorem proving, and agentic robotics, including Code as Policies. My work explores how agents can write code, reason with formal guarantees, and act in the physical world.

I am looking for a research internship in 2027. Get in touch ↗

Before my Ph.D., I completed my B.S. in Computer Science, Mathematics, and Economics at the University of Chicago.
